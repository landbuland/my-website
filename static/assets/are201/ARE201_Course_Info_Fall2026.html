What We Will Learn

1. Economic Thinking

Scarcity, opportunity cost, trade-offs, specialization, and gains from trade.

2. Markets & Prices

Supply and demand, price controls, consumer/producer surplus, and elasticity.

3. Consumers & Firms

Consumer choice, production and costs, perfect competition, and imperfect competition.

4. Policy & the Global Economy

Externalities, public goods, international trade, and real-world agricultural/resource applications.

The goal is to use economic models to explain, graph, calculate, and evaluate real-world decisions and policy trade-offs.

Best strategy

Keep up weekly, practice graphs and calculations, come ready to work, and ask questions early.
