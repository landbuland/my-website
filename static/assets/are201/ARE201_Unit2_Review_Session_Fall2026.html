Sunk Cost

A sunk cost has already been incurred and cannot be recovered. It should be ignored when making the current decision.

Opportunity Cost

Opportunity cost is the net value of the next-best feasible alternative forgone when a choice is made.

Concept review

Farm Share

Farm share is the percentage of the retail food dollar attributable to the farm sector.
Farm-sector amount = retail price × farm-share percentage
Farm share is not farmer profit.
Practice · Farm Share
