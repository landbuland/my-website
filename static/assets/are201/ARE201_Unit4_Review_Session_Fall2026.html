Movement along coffee demandCoffee's own current price is on the vertical axis.

Current coffee demand shifts rightSome buyers purchase now instead of waiting for the higher future price.

Before you leave

Four habits prevent most mistakes

Run the same-price test

If quantity changes at the same price, the curve shifted. A change in the good's own current price moves the point.

POINT OR CURVE?

Name the market first

The price of one good can be on the axis in its own market and a shifter in another market.

WHICH MARKET?

Separate cause from outcome

A shifter moves a curve. The new intersection determines equilibrium price and quantity.

SHIFT → EQUILIBRIUM

For two shocks, combine last

Analyze each shift separately. A variable is ambiguous when the two separate effects point in opposite directions.
