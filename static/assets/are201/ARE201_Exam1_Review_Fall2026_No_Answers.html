Welfare · explain who gains and who loses

A transfer is not a deadweight loss

Same market: P = 30 − 2Q and P = 6 + Q. No government purchases.

OutcomeQ tradedCSPSTSDWL
Free market · P = $14________________________________________
Ceiling · P = $10________________________________________

Transfer

A different price redistributes the gains on units that still trade.

One side’s gain is the other’s loss.

Lost trade

A buyer values a unit more than it costs, but the transaction no longer occurs.

The potential total gain disappears.

“All producers benefit” is too strong.
A higher floor helps some sellers who sell, but excludes others.

A ceiling helps some buyers but excludes others. Lines or lotteries may ration access.

Areas assume the highest-WTP buyers and lowest-cost sellers trade. Other rationing can reduce surplus further.
